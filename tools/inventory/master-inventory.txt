ZenFlow Marketplace: Master Inventory
The Operating System for the Agentic Economy

Executive Summary
This document contains the complete architectural blueprint for the ZenFlow Marketplace. It
details 400 distinct assets categorized into four pillars:
  1.​ Agents (The Workforce): Autonomous entities that reason and execute.
  2.​ Workflows (The Nervous System): Logic chains connecting apps and services.
  3.​ Bots (The Interface): Interactive personalities for user engagement.
  4.​ Databases (The Context): Structured knowledge to ground AI decision-making.
